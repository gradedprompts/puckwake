# Puckwake

An air table hockey game in one HTML file, built by a single prompt in Google Antigravity (Gemini 4 Argon).

Play it: https://gradedprompts.github.io/puckwake/

`index.html` is the unedited output of one run of the prompt. Nothing was changed by hand after the run.
SHA-256: `6ac4682a2e038c8c538ceb781ac072961f744185afbc51f98b830b235b1298dd`

How it plays: two round mallets and one puck on a bright air table seen from above; first to 7 goals wins. Play the computer at Easy, Normal or Hard, or two players on one screen: two people at one keyboard, or two fingers on one phone or tablet laid flat between them, each on their own half. The table turns with the window, so on a phone held upright your goal is at the bottom and the top player's score and callouts face them. Your record at each level is saved in your browser.

Against the computer, the mouse, W A S D or the arrow keys move your mallet, or drag a finger anywhere on the table. With two players, W A S D drive orange and the arrow keys drive blue. P or Esc pauses, M toggles sound. It needs no network at all. Add `?mode=hard` or `?mode=duo` to the address to start a match straight away.

The prompt that built it is being prepared for Graded Prompts: https://www.gradedprompts.com/prompts/apps
